What happens

  1. Book, then check your email.Your confirmation has the door code. Entrances are on Southport and on Clybourn, and the studio is on the second floor.
  2. Arrive ten minutes early.Check in at the desk and put your things in a locker. Go barefoot or wear grip socks, which the desk sells. No shoes in the studio.
  3. Your instructor sets up your reformer.Tell them it's your first time. They'll show you the springs, straps and footbar before class starts.
  4. Class is 45 minutes.The room is kept at 75 to 85 degrees. That is warm enough to sweat and well short of hot yoga.
  5. Shower here if you like.Towels, shampoo and conditioner are provided. You can also book the infrared sauna for afterwards.

Policies

Cancelling a class

Free up to 12 hours before class. After that, or if you don't show, it's a $25 fee. If we change the instructor within 12 hours, text us and we'll cancel it at no charge.

Cancelling a membership

Email info@lincolnparkpilates.com with 30 days' notice. Plans with a 3-month minimum can be cancelled with 30 days' notice once the three months are up.

Unused classes

Membership classes are for the month they come in and don't roll over. If you miss one, you can swap it for a sauna session, once.

Bringing a friend

Members can bring a first-time Chicago friend for $15 and any other guest for $25.

Referrals

Refer a friend who becomes a member and get $100 off, applied in their second month.

Small classes

A class with three or fewer people booked may be cancelled before it starts. We'll email or text you.
